# My guide

Built by the assistant from the shared research (`research/topics/`) and your profile (`personal/profile.md`), using `playbooks/11-personal-guide.md`. Don't edit these files by hand; change your profile or tell the assistant, and it rebuilds them. Lines from your preferences are marked **(your choice)**; lines your clinician should confirm are marked **(check with your clinician)**.

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `diet-guide.md` | What to eat, how much, what to limit, organized by meals, shopping and eating out |
| `targets.md` | Your clinical targets and daily nutrition targets, and what to expect |
| `tracking-targets.csv` | The daily targets in a form you can enter into a tracking app |
| `recipe-prompt.md` | A self-contained prompt for adapting any recipe, usable in any AI |
| `BUILD.yaml` | Which research versions and profile date this guide was built from |

The exact sections inside each file follow the research that exists; they aren't fixed in advance.

> Educational material, not medical advice. Medication decisions belong to your prescriber.
