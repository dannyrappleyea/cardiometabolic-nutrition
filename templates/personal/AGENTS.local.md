## Privacy settings (defaults; edit if you want them looser or stricter)

1. **No exact lab values, blood pressure readings or medication doses in any file.** Prefer "at goal: yes/no". Where a band is needed: A1C 0.5 points; LDL, HDL, total cholesterol, ApoB 10 mg/dL; non-HDL 20 mg/dL; triglycerides 50 mg/dL below 200 and 100 at 200+; BP systolic 10 / diastolic 5; kidney, liver and Lp(a) by category only.
2. **Medications:** generic names only, no brands or doses; describe dose status qualitatively ("being titrated", "stable").
3. **Allowed:** sex, height, weight, age band, food preferences, activity level.
4. **Never record:** names of people, birth dates, exact age, provider/clinic/pharmacy names, locations, insurance, or calendar dates of visits or results. Use "Month N". Restaurant names are allowed; addresses and visit dates are not.
5. If exact values come up in conversation, use them to answer, store only bands, and say so.
