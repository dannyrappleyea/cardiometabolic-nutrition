# 13 — Propose an upstream change from a personal copy (personal)

Personal use is the best detector of gaps: questions the research can't answer, rules that are unclear in practice, or sources that look outdated. This playbook turns one into an upstream request **without any personal data**.

## Steps

1. **Generalize the question** to the cohort. "Is X OK with my medication and my kidney numbers?" becomes "How does X interact with <medication class>, and does kidney function change that?" Remove everything about the person: no lab values or bands, medications as classes only if needed, no tastes, routines, names, places or dates.
2. **Check it's new:** search `research/_plan/topic-map.yaml`, topic questions and open upstream issues.
3. **Draft an issue** using `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/topic-request.md`: the generalized question, why it matters for the cohort (SCOPE §3–4), and any Tier 1–3 sources found (titles and links only).
4. **Show the person the exact text** and get a clear yes before they (or you, if they ask and you have the tools) post it. Posting publicly is their decision.
5. Don't write research files in the personal copy. If they want an interim answer, give it in conversation and note it isn't reviewed research.
