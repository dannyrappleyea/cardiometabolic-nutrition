# Discovery log

Written by `playbooks/01-discover.md`. One row per search or document consulted, so a reviewer can see how each topic was found. Empty until the first discovery run.

**Access** says how much was actually seen: `full`, `partial`, `section`, `abstract`, `summary`, `landing-page` (opened, with a source file), or `snippet` (search results only). Snippet rows can suggest searches and `watch` items; they never justify a topic or question on their own (METHODS §1).

**Candidates (as found)** keeps the wording the document used; **Topic IDs** maps them to topic-map IDs.
